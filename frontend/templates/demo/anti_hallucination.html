Patient Demographics

The subject is a 72-year-old male diagnosed with metastatic renal cell carcinoma who was enrolled in the Phase III EV-302 trial. The patient had an ECOG performance status of 1 and a history of hepatic impairment.

Treatment

The patient received Padcev (enfortumab vedotin) at a dose of 1.5 mg/kg intravenously on Days 1, 8, and 15 of each 28-day cycle, in combination with pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every 3 weeks. The subject completed 6 cycles of treatment before the adverse event occurred.

Adverse Event Description

On Day 47 (Cycle 3, Day 5), the patient developed Grade 2 maculopapular rash affecting approximately 15% BSA, primarily on the trunk and upper extremities. The rash was accompanied by pruritus. Initial management included topical corticosteroids (triamcinolone 0.1% cream BID).

By Day 52, the rash progressed to Grade 3 with BSA involvement of approximately 40%, extending to the lower extremities. The patient was started on oral prednisone 1 mg/kg/day. Treatment was continued without modification.

Outcome

The maculopapular rash resolved to Grade 1 by Day 68 with corticosteroid taper. Padcev was restarted at full dose on Day 71 (Cycle 4, Day 1). No recurrence of Grade 3+ rash was observed during subsequent cycles. The event was assessed as related to enfortumab vedotin.
